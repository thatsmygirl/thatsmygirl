# Hyeonjin Jang

M.S. student in Computer Engineering at **Dongseo University**, Busan, South Korea.

I work on **reinforcement learning for energy and networked systems** — teaching agents to make sequential decisions (battery/microturbine dispatch, traffic control, resource allocation) under realistic dynamics and uncertainty.

---

### Research interests

- Deep reinforcement learning (SAC, TD3, PPO, DDPG) for sequential decision-making
- Microgrid energy management systems and battery storage (nonlinear losses & degradation)
- RL for networked systems: traffic control, satellite resource allocation, federated learning
