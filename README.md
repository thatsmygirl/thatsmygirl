# Hyeonjin Jang

M.S. student in Computer Engineering at **Dongseo University**, Busan, South Korea —
**AA (AI and its Applications) Lab**, advised by Prof. **Sunyong Kim**.

I lead research on **reinforcement learning for energy and networked systems**: formulating real-world operation problems as Markov decision processes and solving them with deep RL under realistic dynamics and uncertainty.

### Research Interests

- Deep reinforcement learning (SAC, TD3, PPO, DDPG) for sequential decision-making
- Microgrid energy management & battery storage (nonlinear losses, cyclic degradation)
- RL for networked systems: traffic control, satellite resource allocation, federated learning
- Brain–computer interfaces: EEG-based emotion classification (undergraduate research)

### Research Experience

*I led the work below end-to-end — problem formulation, modeling, experiments, and writing — except where noted.*

- **Microgrid Energy Management with Deep RL** — AA Lab, Dongseo University · *manuscript under review*
  A soft actor-critic (SAC) EMS for a PV–microturbine–battery microgrid modeled with nonlinear charging/discharging losses and cyclic degradation; benchmarked against TD3, PPO, DDPG, and a MILP optimum under both ToU and RTP tariffs. → [`microgrid-drl-ems`](https://github.com/thatsmygirl/microgrid-drl-ems)
- **RL-based Variable Speed Limit for Highway Congestion** — modeled the on-ramp capacity drop and learned a variable-speed-limit policy to relieve congestion (Journal of KIECS, 2026).
- **CGAN-based Data Augmentation for Non-IID Federated Learning** — tackled the Non-IID data problem in federated learning with CGAN-generated samples (Journal of KIECS, 2025).
- **Cooperative-Robot Dynamic AI Task Control** — built and evaluated a testbed for dynamic AI task control of collaborative robots (KICS conference, 2024).
- **DRL-based Cooperative Resource Allocation for LEO Satellites** — proposed a deep-RL scheme for cooperative resource allocation among LEO satellites (concept study; KICS conference, 2023).
- **Undergraduate Researcher — Brain–Computer Interface** — EEG-based emotion classification.
