# Hyeonjin Jang

M.S. student in Computer Engineering at **Dongseo University**, Busan, South Korea.

I work on **reinforcement learning for energy systems** — teaching agents to operate batteries, microturbines, and grid exchange cost-effectively under realistic physics and uncertain electricity prices.

---

### Research interests

- Deep reinforcement learning (SAC, TD3, PPO, DDPG) for sequential decision-making
- Microgrid energy management systems (EMS)
- Battery energy storage: nonlinear losses and cyclic degradation modeling
- Computer graphics

### Current work

- **Microgrid EMS with deep reinforcement learning** under time-of-use and real-time pricing — *manuscript under review*.
